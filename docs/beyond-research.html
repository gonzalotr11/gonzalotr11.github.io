Beyond Research

My work extends beyond academic research. Over the last decade, I have worked across civil society organizations, consulting, public policy, and public writing, developing applied knowledge on education, poverty, work, and social inequality.

Civil Society and Social Intervention

Research and Development in Civil Society

Fundación INFOCAP (2018–2024)

I worked in research and institutional development in a civil society organization focused on labor training, education, and social inclusion. My work included internal and external analysis, institutional development proposals, research reports, and evidence-based support for programs addressing vulnerability, work, and adult education.

Consulting and Policy Reports

Advanced Quantitative Analysis for Social Well-Being

United Nations Development Programme, Chile

Independent consultant for the analysis of the Social Well-Being Survey of the Ministry of Social Development and Family, using advanced quantitative methods.

Evaluation of Education Research Proposals

Ministry of Education, Chile — FONIDE 2023

Technical evaluator for proposals submitted to the Education Research and Development Fund. The evaluation considered methodological quality, internal coherence, and alignment with public policy priorities in education.

Higher Education, Science, and Innovation Policy

National Council for Science, Technology, Knowledge and Innovation for Development (Consejo CTCI), 2025

Author of the report Recommendations of the CTCI Council in Higher Education: A Systematization of Contributions from Recent Decades, focused on synthesizing policy recommendations in higher education, science, technology, knowledge, and innovation.
